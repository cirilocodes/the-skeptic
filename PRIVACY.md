# The Skeptic: privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

The Skeptic is a Forge app and Rovo agent for Jira and Confluence, made by Cyril Akakpo as an individual. This page says what the app reads, what it keeps, and where that data stays.

## Where your data lives

The Skeptic runs entirely on Atlassian Forge. It has no servers of its own. Everything it keeps sits in Forge storage on your Atlassian site. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any outside service.

All AI work goes through Atlassian Rovo, under your site's own Rovo terms. The Skeptic does not call any other AI provider.

## What it reads

- Jira work items and Confluence pages, to find past plans, incidents and decisions that bear on a new plan. It reads them as the person who asked for the review, or as the app for background reviews, and only within the permissions your site gives it.
- Atlassian account IDs: who asked for a review, who owns a plan and who signs off.

## What it keeps

- Reviews: the points raised, the evidence they cite and the answers given.
- A search index of past items (keys, titles and short text), so reviews are fast.
- Settings chosen by a site admin.
- Atlassian account IDs tied to reviews, answers and sign-offs. No names, emails or other profile details are kept.

## Who can see it

A review only cites items the person viewing it is allowed to see. Comments the app posts in the background give counts or item keys only.

## How long it is kept

Data stays until an admin clears it from The Skeptic settings page, or until the app is uninstalled. When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes its Forge storage.
